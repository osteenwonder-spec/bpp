How BPP Works

1. Join a Group: Log in and find a bulk order in your neighborhood.

2. Add Your Demand: Say how much you want to buy at the wholesale price.

3. Pay Directly: You pay the supplier directly via MoMo or cash. BPP never holds your money.

4. Save Money: Collect your goods at the group drop-off point and enjoy the savings!

About BPP

BPP (Bulk Purchase Platform) is a Rwandan startup designed to fight the rising cost of living. By digitizing the traditional community buying club, we help families access wholesale prices without the need for middlemen.

We do not hold funds. We simply provide the ledger and coordination tools so communities can trust each other and save together.
